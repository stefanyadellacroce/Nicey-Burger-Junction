Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Choose items from the Menu, customize them, add them to your cart and proceed through checkout.

Can I order for pickup or delivery?

Yes, when each fulfillment option is enabled in Website Settings. Delivery is limited to active delivery zones.

How do manual digital payments work?

Place the order, follow the payment instructions, then submit your reference number and proof image. Staff verifies the payment before it is marked paid.

Can I cancel an order?

Customer cancellation is allowed only while the order is eligible under the configured rules. Staff can record cancellations with reasons; orders are never silently deleted.
