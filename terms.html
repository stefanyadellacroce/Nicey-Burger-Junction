Ordering Policy

Prices, availability, delivery fees and promotion eligibility are validated by the database when an order is created. Browser totals are estimates until the secure order transaction succeeds.

Payments

Manual GCash, Maya and bank-transfer methods require a valid reference and proof submission when enabled. A payment is not treated as verified until authorized staff verifies it.

Cancellations

Cancellation eligibility depends on the current order state. Cancelled orders remain in history and may trigger inventory reversal when stock had already been deducted.
