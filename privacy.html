Privacy Policy

Nicey Burger Junction uses account, contact, address, order and payment-submission information only to operate ordering and customer support. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Payment proof files are stored in a private bucket and access is limited by authorization rules. Administrative secrets are never stored in browser JavaScript.

Data controls

Customers can update profile details and saved addresses from their account. Operational records such as completed orders, stock movements, payment verification and audit logs are retained for legitimate business and accounting purposes.
